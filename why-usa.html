Why invest in the USA?

A green card brings your family the freedom to live, study and work anywhere in the United States — and EB-5 lets you earn it by funding projects the country needs.

What a green card gives your family

Live anywhere

Settle in any state, with the right to travel in and out of the US.

Work or build a business

No employer sponsorship or H-1B lottery; change jobs or start a company freely.

Education

Children attend US schools, and green card holders often qualify for in-state tuition at public universities.

Path to citizenship

Apply for US citizenship after five years as a permanent resident, subject to eligibility.

Why renewable energy, why Texas

Texas runs its own power grid (ERCOT), has abundant sun and open land, and is among the fastest-growing solar and battery-storage markets in the United States. Rising demand from data centers, industry and population growth keeps the need for new generation high.

Energy and infrastructure projects are capital-intensive and create construction and operating jobs — the two things EB-5 is designed to support. Qualifying infrastructure projects also have a dedicated 2% visa reserve.
