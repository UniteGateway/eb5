Is EB-5 suitable for you?

EB-5 suits families who want permanent residency, can commit capital for roughly five or more years, and can document where their money came from.

Key risks

Capital at risk
Projects can underperform or fail; repayment is not guaranteed.
Immigration risk
USCIS may deny a petition, for example if job creation or source of funds falls short.
Visa availability
Backlogs or retrogression can delay the final green card.
Liquidity
Capital is typically locked in for five or more years.
Program changes
Congress and USCIS can change rules, amounts and authorisation.
Currency
INR–USD movements affect the rupee value of your return.
