About Unite Group

GoEB5 is powered by Unite Group, Inc. — a diversified international group across renewable energy, technology, construction, fintech and consultancy, with operations in the USA, the UK and India.

A renewable energy group building across three continents

Unite Group works across solar EPC, rooftop and ground-mounted projects, OEM module manufacturing, battery energy storage and renewable energy parks. Its India operations are led from Hyderabad, serving Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and beyond.

GoEB5 brings that experience to US investors: a dedicated desk that helps Indian and global families understand the EB-5 program and evaluate US renewable-energy and infrastructure opportunities — starting with a proposed solar project in Texas.

What we do

Solar EPC

Design, engineering, procurement and construction for rooftop, ground-mounted and utility-scale solar.

Storage & energy parks

Solar-plus-BESS parks and hybrid renewable projects for industrial and utility offtakers.

Manufacturing & supply

OEM solar module manufacturing and multi-country procurement of balance-of-system equipment.
