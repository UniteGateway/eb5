FAQ & resources

Straight answers, plus links to official US government sources.

Common questions

What is the minimum investment today?

$800,000 in a targeted employment area or infrastructure project, or $1,050,000 elsewhere. On 1 January 2027 both rise by a statutory inflation formula; the TEA amount is projected near $937,500.

How long does it take?

I-526E petitions commonly take 12–24 months. Set-aside categories are currently available for India, which can shorten the wait for the visa itself.

Is my capital guaranteed back?

No. EB-5 law requires capital to be "at risk". Projects plan an exit after the sustainment period, but repayment is never guaranteed — read each offering's risk section.

Why solar and infrastructure?

Both create many construction and operating jobs, have long-term contracted revenue, and infrastructure projects qualify for the $800,000 reduced amount and a dedicated 2% visa reserve.

Do I need my own lawyer?

Yes. We coordinate the work, but every investor should retain independent US immigration counsel and, where relevant, a chartered accountant for LRS and tax.

Can my children study in the US meanwhile?

Yes. Children under 21 are included as derivatives; once approved they pay in-state tuition at many public universities.

Is the Texas project open for investment?

Not yet. It is at concept and feasibility stage; EB-5 eligibility, structuring and offering documents are still being prepared.

Which languages do you support?

English today, with Hindi and Telugu consultations available on request and website translations planned.
