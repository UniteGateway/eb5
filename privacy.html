Privacy Policy

How GoEB5 collects, uses and protects your information.

Information we collect

Details you submit in our forms: name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, country, investment readiness, timeline, family details and messages.

Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the site working and secure.

How we use it

To respond to enquiries, schedule consultations, send requested brochures and project updates, and assess general suitability for the EB-5 program.

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

With your consent, we may share your details with independent immigration counsel, regional centers or project sponsors you choose to engage, and with service providers who host our systems under confidentiality obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or stop contacting you, by writing to info@goeb5.in.

We follow applicable data protection law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards and keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.
