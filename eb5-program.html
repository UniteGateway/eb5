The EB-5 Visa Program

Eligibility, investment thresholds, job requirements and key dates — the EB-5 education center.

Under the EB-5 Reform and Integrity Act of 2022. The 2027 TEA minimum is projected near $937,500; USCIS has not yet published final figures. Program authorised through 30 September 2027.

Who is eligible

  • Any foreign national aged 18+ who invests the required capital.
  • Funds must come from a lawful, documented source (salary, business income, property sale, gift or loan).
  • Capital must be "at risk" in a new commercial enterprise.
  • The investment must create at least 10 full-time jobs for US workers.
  • Your spouse and unmarried children under 21 receive green cards too.

Two ways to invest

Regional center (indirect)

Pooled investment sponsored by a USCIS-designated regional center. Indirect and induced jobs count, and you have no day-to-day management duty. Investors file Form I-526E.

Direct investment

You invest in and help manage your own business. Only direct W-2 jobs count. Investors file Form I-526.

Reserved (set-aside) visas

The 2022 reform reserves 32% of EB-5 visas each year: 20% for rural projects, 10% for high-unemployment areas and 2% for infrastructure projects. These set-aside categories have recently been available for India even while the unreserved category is backlogged.

Key terms

TEA

Targeted Employment Area — a rural area or one with unemployment at least 150% of the national average; qualifies for the reduced minimum.

I-526E

The investor petition for regional-center investments, proving lawful funds and a qualifying investment.

I-829

Petition to remove conditions after about two years, showing the jobs were created and capital sustained.

PPM

Private placement memorandum — the legal offering document describing the project, risks and terms.

At risk

Capital must be exposed to gain or loss; guaranteed returns are not permitted.

Visa Bulletin

Monthly State Department chart showing when each category and country can take the final step.
