Texas Solar Project — McAllen Area

A proposed solar development with potential battery energy storage on a 112-acre site in South Texas.

Project concept

The concept is a ground-mounted solar plant on single-axis trackers, with battery energy storage evaluated to shift power into evening peak hours on the ERCOT grid. Unite Solar USA would deliver engineering, procurement and construction, drawing on Unite Group's multi-country supply chain.

Final capacity, storage size and interconnection point will be fixed by the feasibility and grid studies now being planned.

Project milestones

  1. Site identifiedComplete112-acre property in the McAllen area, Texas.
  2. Local partnershipIn progressArrangement with 112 Citrus LLC for land rights and local development.
  3. Technical feasibilityPlannedSolar resource, layout, battery storage sizing and grid interconnection studies.
  4. EB-5 structuringPlannedTEA / infrastructure qualification review, job-creation model and regional-center sponsorship.

Important to know

The 112-acre property does not automatically qualify for the reduced $800,000 amount or a rural / TEA designation. Eligibility, the job-creation model, regional-center sponsorship, land rights and the offering structure must be independently verified before any offering is made.
